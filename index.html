Architecture & Mission

What is ISL Real-Time Translator?

Unlike commercial AI that focuses exclusively on Western American Sign Language (ASL), this platform is purpose-built for the unique grammar, dual-handed fingerspelling, and regional vocabulary of Indian Sign Language (ISL).

01

RGB Video Stream

Captures live 30 FPS video through any standard mobile or laptop camera without expensive data gloves or depth sensors.

02

MediaPipe Landmark Tracking

Extracts 21 three-dimensional (X, Y, Z) coordinates per hand, locating wrist anchors, knuckles, and fingertip positions.

03

Spatial Normalization & LSTM

Normalizes coordinates relative to the wrist anchor to eliminate camera distance bias, classified via a temporal neural network.

04

Hindi Speech & Text Output

Converts identified gestures into natural Hindi voice synthesis (gTTS) and bilingual subtitles for instantaneous conversation.

Indian Sign Language (ISL)

  • Primarily two-handed alphabet and gesture configurations.
  • Distinct SOV (Subject-Object-Verb) grammatical framework.
  • Rich cultural markers for Indian courtesies (Namaste, Pranam, Dhanyawaad).
  • Native visual language for 63M+ deaf Indians.

American Sign Language (ASL)

  • Primarily single-handed fingerspelling system.
  • SVO (Subject-Verb-Object) sentence structure.
  • Western cultural gestures incompatible with Indian dialects.
  • Fails to interpret everyday Indian social communication.

Hello / Namaste

नमस्ते (Namaste)

The universal Indian greeting of deep respect. Hands brought together palm-to-palm in front of chest.

How to Sign Properly:

Bring both palms together at chest level with fingers pointing upward. Tilt head gently forward with a welcoming smile.

Cultural Etiquette:

In Indian Sign Language, Namaste incorporates hands touching with open palms facing inward, symbolizing honor and equality.

Deploy Instantly to Vercel (100% Free)

This dedicated web experience is pre-configured with vercel.json and zero backend build steps. It deploys directly to Vercel's global CDN in seconds!

  1. Push this project to your GitHub account: git push origin main
  2. Go to vercel.com/new and connect your GitHub repo.
  3. Click Deploy! Vercel automatically hosts the static assets worldwide for free.
