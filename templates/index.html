नमस्ते (Namaste)

The universal Indian greeting of deep respect. Hands brought together palm-to-palm in front of chest.

How to Sign:

Bring both palms together at chest level with fingers pointing upward.

Cultural Context:

In Indian Sign Language, Namaste incorporates two hands touching with open palms.

Project Workflow

How the neural pipeline transforms live video frames into spoken language in milliseconds.

01

Hand Tracking

MediaPipe locates and tracks 21 three-dimensional hand landmarks per frame in real time.

02

Data Collection

Landmark coordinates are normalized relative to the wrist anchor and labeled for each gesture class.

03

Model Training

A deep-learning CNN + LSTM model learns to classify temporal gesture sequences over a 30-frame window.

04

Real-Time Detection

The trained model predicts gestures live and triggers instant bilingual text + spoken Hindi audio.
